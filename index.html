CNS-Sparing Recomp Rules

1. TWO WORKING SETS ONLY

Execute 1 heavy Top Set (6–8 or 8–10 reps) followed by 1 lighter Back-Off Set (10–12 or 12–15 reps). Slicing junk volume preserves mental capital.

2. STRICT RPE 8.5 CAP

Always leave 1 to 2 clean reps in the tank. Never grind to complete failure on compound lifts.
